ScoreAssist: AI Pre-Scoring for Sleep Studies

Sleep technologists score every study by hand, one 30-second window at a time. ScoreAssist lets an AI model score the whole night first, then sends only the windows it is least sure about to the technologist. Tested on 30 real nights scored by sleep experts (Sleep-EDF, PhysioNet).
